Analyst notes

The write-up

Example notes, written to show the format. Yours go here.

Headline

Cleveland won Game 7 on the offensive glass. They rebounded 43.5% of their own misses (20 offensive rebounds) to Toronto's 14.9% (7), a gap worth about 14.5 points per 100 possessions, more than the 12-point final margin.

What I noticed

  • The series turned in five minutes around halftime. Toronto led 49-43 with 2:04 left in the second quarter and didn't score again until 9:14 of the third. Cleveland's 15-0 run (two threes in the last 71 seconds of the half, then nine straight points after it) flipped a 6-point lead into a 9-point deficit. The third quarter finished 38-19.
  • Jarrett Allen was the difference inside: 8 offensive rebounds, 19 total, 14 free-throw attempts, and a Game Score of 25.3, the best on the floor. To check on film: were his putbacks from Toronto's bigs getting pulled out to help on drives, or from losing box-outs?
  • The shots went to the less efficient scorer. RJ Barrett took 25 shots for 23 points (42% true shooting against 58% on the season) with a 30% usage rate. Scottie Barnes scored 24 on 14 shots and went 7-for-7 from the line. In an elimination game, Barnes finishing only 23% of Toronto's plays is the decision worth questioning.

What to watch next season

Toronto scored 19 points in the quarter that decided the series. Does Kawhi Leonard fix that half-court scoring drought? Track the Raptors' offensive rating in third quarters and in clutch time over the first month, against 2025-26.
